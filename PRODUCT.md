# Chapterloom product direction

## Audience

People organizing personal audiobook files for BookPlayer or another chapter-aware reader.

## Core job

Turn one long audio file with spoken chapter headings into a chaptered M4B. Keep existing book tags and cover art. Let listener verify every detected boundary before saving.

## Experience

Three visible steps: source audio, find chapters, review and export. Local processing and progress remain visible. Timing adjustments and preview sit directly beside each marker.

## Scope

Native macOS utility for M4A, M4B, and MP3. English spoken chapter headings. Local Whisper transcription. Manual marker edits. M4B export and chapter verification.

## Known limits

Whisper can miss or mishear headings. Review remains essential. MP3 must be encoded to AAC for M4B export. First 30 min scan still exports entire source file.
